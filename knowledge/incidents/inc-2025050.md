# inc-2025050: Rising ECC errors on a100-node-02 GPU 4

## Summary
XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 4 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors. Severity sev2. Resolved in 42.4 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-11 12:46 UTC: XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 4 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors.
- 13:20 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-13 07:13 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degrading HBM stack on GPU 4 (serial 1665859814648).

## Resolution
Reset applied pending row remaps; errors resumed within 2 days; GPU replaced under RMA.

## Lessons
Errors resuming after a reset is the RMA signal; don't keep resetting.
