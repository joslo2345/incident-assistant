# inc-2025043: Rising ECC errors on a100-node-01 GPU 4

## Summary
XID 92 (high single-bit ECC error rate) on GPU 4; 172 correctable errors in the last hour. Severity sev2. Resolved in 29.1 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-12-17 04:15 UTC: XID 92 (high single-bit ECC error rate) on GPU 4; 172 correctable errors in the last hour.
- 04:47 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-12-18 09:19 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Single bad row on GPU 4; pending remap had not been applied.

## Resolution
Drained and reset the GPU; remap applied; no further errors over 30 days.

## Lessons
Check remapped_rows.pending first; a reset alone often fixes an isolated row.
