# inc-2025058: Power capping on a100-node-01 after PSU loss

## Summary
Jobs on a100-node-01 slowed by 40%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit. Severity sev2. Resolved in 2.2 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-09-26 07:21 UTC: Jobs on a100-node-01 slowed by 40%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit.
- 07:53 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-09-26 09:30 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
PSU3 module failed.

## Resolution
Hot-swapped PSU3 from spares; enforced limits back to default.

## Lessons
No drain needed for a PSU swap on redundant chassis.
