# inc-2025011: NVLink errors between GPUs 0-1 on a100-node-03

## Summary
NVLink CRC errors climbed to 145 per minute between GPUs 0-1; all-reduce time doubled. Severity sev2. Resolved in 13.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-09-22 17:18 UTC: NVLink CRC errors climbed to 145 per minute between GPUs 0-1; all-reduce time doubled.
- 17:52 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-09-23 07:15 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty NVSwitch port on the baseboard.

## Resolution
Errors returned after reset; baseboard replaced by the vendor.

## Lessons
Attach nvidia-smi nvlink -e counters from both GPUs to the RMA.
