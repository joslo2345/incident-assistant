# inc-2025054: PCIe replays on h100-node-01 GPU 7

## Summary
GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing. Severity sev3. Resolved in 12.6 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-05-15 03:33 UTC: GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing.
- 03:40 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-05-15 16:09 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degraded PCIe cable to the retimer.

## Resolution
Replaced the cable; replays stopped.

## Lessons
Replays on one GPU only point at its own path, not the PCIe switch.
