# inc-2025000: Thermal throttling on a100-node-01 GPUs 0-3

## Summary
BMC reported FAN1 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 5.1 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-03 12:50 UTC: BMC reported FAN1 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown.
- 13:09 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-03 17:58 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN1 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN1, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
