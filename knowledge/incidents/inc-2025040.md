# inc-2025040: PCIe replays on h100-node-04 GPU 2

## Summary
PCIe replay counter on GPU 2 rising 200 per minute; data loading slowed. Severity sev3. Resolved in 22.5 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-05-01 08:37 UTC: PCIe replay counter on GPU 2 rising 200 per minute; data loading slowed.
- 09:11 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-05-02 07:08 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Riser not fully seated after maintenance.

## Resolution
Reseated the riser; link back at x16 Gen5, counter flat.

## Lessons
Check LnkSta against LnkCap after every service visit.
