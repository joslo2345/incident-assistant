# inc-2025056: Thermal throttling on h100-node-02 GPUs 4-7

## Summary
Detector flagged GPUs 4-7 running 13 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected. Severity sev2. Resolved in 1.2 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-05 00:16 UTC: Detector flagged GPUs 4-7 running 13 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected.
- 00:46 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-05 01:27 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Fan connector for FAN4 had worked loose after a previous service visit.

## Resolution
Reseated the connector; fan back to normal RPM.

## Lessons
After any chassis service, check all fan readings before resuming the node.
