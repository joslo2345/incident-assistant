# inc-2025016: Power capping on h100-node-03 after PSU loss

## Summary
Jobs on h100-node-03 slowed by 22%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit. Severity sev2. Resolved in 5.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-03-13 18:22 UTC: Jobs on h100-node-03 slowed by 22%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit.
- 18:48 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-03-14 00:17 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
PSU1 module failed.

## Resolution
Hot-swapped PSU1 from spares; enforced limits back to default.

## Lessons
No drain needed for a PSU swap on redundant chassis.
