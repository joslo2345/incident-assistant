# inc-2025033: PCIe replays on h100-node-02 GPU 7

## Summary
GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing. Severity sev3. Resolved in 9.5 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-23 05:07 UTC: GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing.
- 05:24 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-23 14:35 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Riser not fully seated after maintenance.

## Resolution
Reseated the riser; link back at x16 Gen5, counter flat.

## Lessons
Check LnkSta against LnkCap after every service visit.
