# inc-2025044: Power capping on a100-node-03 after PSU loss

## Summary
All GPUs on a100-node-03 dropped to a 240 W enforced limit; BMC reported PSU3 input lost. Severity sev2. Resolved in 1.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-07 05:30 UTC: All GPUs on a100-node-03 dropped to a 240 W enforced limit; BMC reported PSU3 input lost.
- 05:39 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-07 07:12 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Power cap left in place after a facility event the week before.

## Resolution
Removed the manual cap with nvidia-smi -pl; no hardware fault.

## Lessons
Record every manual cap with an end condition; forgotten caps look like PSU faults.
