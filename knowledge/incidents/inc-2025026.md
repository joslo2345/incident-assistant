# inc-2025026: PCIe replays on h100-node-04 GPU 7

## Summary
GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing. Severity sev3. Resolved in 8.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-04-04 10:21 UTC: GPU 7 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing.
- 10:34 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-04-04 18:21 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Riser not fully seated after maintenance.

## Resolution
Reseated the riser; link back at x16 Gen5, counter flat.

## Lessons
Check LnkSta against LnkCap after every service visit.
