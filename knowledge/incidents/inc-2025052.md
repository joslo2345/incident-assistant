# inc-2025052: GPU 7 fell off the bus on h100-node-03

## Summary
XID 79 on GPU 7; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-03 failed. Severity sev1. Resolved in 8.4 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-01-17 03:55 UTC: XID 79 on GPU 7; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-03 failed.
- 04:07 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-01-17 12:16 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty PCIe riser for GPU 7.

## Resolution
Node rebooted, riser replaced after a second XID 79 a week later.

## Lessons
PCIe replays had been rising on this GPU for days before the first XID 79.
