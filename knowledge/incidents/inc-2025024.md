# inc-2025024: GPU 2 fell off the bus on a100-node-01

## Summary
XID 79 on GPU 2; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on a100-node-01 failed. Severity sev1. Resolved in 30.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-01 02:24 UTC: XID 79 on GPU 2; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on a100-node-01 failed.
- 02:53 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-02 09:21 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty PCIe riser for GPU 2.

## Resolution
Node rebooted, riser replaced after a second XID 79 a week later.

## Lessons
PCIe replays had been rising on this GPU for days before the first XID 79.
