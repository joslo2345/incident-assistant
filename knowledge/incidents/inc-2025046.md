# inc-2025046: NVLink errors between GPUs 4-5 on h100-node-02

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 4; training throughput on h100-node-02 dropped 24%. Severity sev2. Resolved in 32.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-11-20 07:11 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 4; training throughput on h100-node-02 dropped 24%.
- 07:32 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-11-21 15:51 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Link training issue cleared by a baseboard reset.

## Resolution
Drained, reset all GPUs on the baseboard; errors did not return.

## Lessons
On NVSwitch systems, reset the whole baseboard, not one GPU.
