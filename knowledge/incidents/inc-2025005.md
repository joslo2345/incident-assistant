# inc-2025005: PCIe replays on h100-node-03 GPU 6

## Summary
PCIe replay counter on GPU 6 rising 223 per minute; data loading slowed. Severity sev3. Resolved in 1.3 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-25 21:10 UTC: PCIe replay counter on GPU 6 rising 223 per minute; data loading slowed.
- 21:26 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-25 22:27 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Riser not fully seated after maintenance.

## Resolution
Reseated the riser; link back at x16 Gen5, counter flat.

## Lessons
Check LnkSta against LnkCap after every service visit.
