# inc-2025035: Thermal throttling on h100-node-05 GPUs 0-3

## Summary
BMC reported FAN2 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 90 C and entered thermal slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 5.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-03-23 20:08 UTC: BMC reported FAN2 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 90 C and entered thermal slowdown.
- 20:42 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-03-24 02:00 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN2 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN2, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
