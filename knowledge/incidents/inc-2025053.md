# inc-2025053: NVLink errors between GPUs 0-1 on h100-node-03

## Summary
NVLink CRC errors climbed to 277 per minute between GPUs 0-1; all-reduce time doubled. Severity sev2. Resolved in 44.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-06-06 01:17 UTC: NVLink CRC errors climbed to 277 per minute between GPUs 0-1; all-reduce time doubled.
- 01:33 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-06-07 21:17 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty NVSwitch port on the baseboard.

## Resolution
Errors returned after reset; baseboard replaced by the vendor.

## Lessons
Attach nvidia-smi nvlink -e counters from both GPUs to the RMA.
