# inc-2025006: Suspected overheating on h100-node-05 (no fault)

## Summary
On-call paged for high power and temperature on h100-node-05; no errors in any counter. Severity sev4. Resolved in 0.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-05-25 08:02 UTC: On-call paged for high power and temperature on h100-node-05; no errors in any counter.
- 08:11 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-05-25 08:58 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Another team's job was sharing the node and saturating the GPUs.

## Resolution
Moved the job to exclusive nodes via the scheduler; no hardware action.

## Lessons
Noisy neighbors are a scheduling problem, not a hardware one.
