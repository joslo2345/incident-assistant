# inc-2025012: PCIe replays on h100-node-03 GPU 5

## Summary
PCIe replay counter on GPU 5 rising 139 per minute; data loading slowed. Severity sev3. Resolved in 14.5 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-04-18 04:09 UTC: PCIe replay counter on GPU 5 rising 139 per minute; data loading slowed.
- 04:48 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-04-18 18:38 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degraded PCIe cable to the retimer.

## Resolution
Replaced the cable; replays stopped.

## Lessons
Replays on one GPU only point at its own path, not the PCIe switch.
