# inc-2025032: NVLink errors between GPUs 6-7 on h100-node-01

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 6; training throughput on h100-node-01 dropped 28%. Severity sev3. Resolved in 13.3 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-06-27 00:43 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 6; training throughput on h100-node-01 dropped 28%.
- 00:58 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-06-27 13:59 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Link training issue cleared by a baseboard reset.

## Resolution
Drained, reset all GPUs on the baseboard; errors did not return.

## Lessons
On NVSwitch systems, reset the whole baseboard, not one GPU.
