# inc-2025014: Thermal throttling on h100-node-01 GPUs 4-7

## Summary
Training step time rose 14% on a job using h100-node-01; GPUs 4-7 showed hw_thermal_slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 4.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-04-30 19:44 UTC: Training step time rose 14% on a job using h100-node-01; GPUs 4-7 showed hw_thermal_slowdown.
- 20:06 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-04-30 23:46 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN3 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN3, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
