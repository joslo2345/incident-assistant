# inc-2025025: NVLink errors between GPUs 2-3 on a100-node-03

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on a100-node-03 dropped 16%. Severity sev3. Resolved in 27.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-04-03 03:00 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on a100-node-03 dropped 16%.
- 03:18 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-04-04 05:57 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Link training issue cleared by a baseboard reset.

## Resolution
Drained, reset all GPUs on the baseboard; errors did not return.

## Lessons
On NVSwitch systems, reset the whole baseboard, not one GPU.
