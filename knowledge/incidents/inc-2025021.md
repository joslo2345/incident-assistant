# inc-2025021: Thermal throttling on h100-node-04 GPUs 0-3

## Summary
BMC reported FAN1 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 3.5 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-09-01 04:32 UTC: BMC reported FAN1 at 0 RPM; GPUs 0-3 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown.
- 04:52 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-09-01 08:03 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN1 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN1, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
