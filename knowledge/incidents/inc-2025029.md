# inc-2025029: Rising ECC errors on h100-node-04 GPU 7

## Summary
XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 7 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors. Severity sev2. Resolved in 24.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-14 11:07 UTC: XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 7 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors.
- 11:37 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-15 11:50 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Single bad row on GPU 7; pending remap had not been applied.

## Resolution
Drained and reset the GPU; remap applied; no further errors over 30 days.

## Lessons
Check remapped_rows.pending first; a reset alone often fixes an isolated row.
