# inc-2025060: NVLink errors between GPUs 2-3 on a100-node-01

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on a100-node-01 dropped 40%. Severity sev2. Resolved in 79.2 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-12-20 01:27 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on a100-node-01 dropped 40%.
- 02:00 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-12-23 08:36 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty NVSwitch port on the baseboard.

## Resolution
Errors returned after reset; baseboard replaced by the vendor.

## Lessons
Attach nvidia-smi nvlink -e counters from both GPUs to the RMA.
