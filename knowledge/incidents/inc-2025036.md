# inc-2025036: Rising ECC errors on h100-node-02 GPU 0

## Summary
XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 0 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors. Severity sev2. Resolved in 29.2 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-12 00:03 UTC: XID 48 (double-bit ECC error) on GPU 0 killed a training job after a day of rising correctable errors.
- 00:08 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-13 05:13 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degrading HBM stack on GPU 0 (serial 1623366508117).

## Resolution
Reset applied pending row remaps; errors resumed within 2 days; GPU replaced under RMA.

## Lessons
Errors resuming after a reset is the RMA signal; don't keep resetting.
