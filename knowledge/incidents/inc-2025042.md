# inc-2025042: Thermal throttling on h100-node-01 GPUs 4-7

## Summary
BMC reported FAN4 at 0 RPM; GPUs 4-7 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 1.3 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-06-20 04:21 UTC: BMC reported FAN4 at 0 RPM; GPUs 4-7 reached 88 C and entered thermal slowdown.
- 04:30 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-06-20 05:40 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Fan connector for FAN4 had worked loose after a previous service visit.

## Resolution
Reseated the connector; fan back to normal RPM.

## Lessons
After any chassis service, check all fan readings before resuming the node.
