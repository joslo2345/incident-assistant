# inc-2025015: Rising ECC errors on a100-node-03 GPU 3

## Summary
Correctable ECC errors on GPU 3 rose to 220 in 30 minutes; remapped rows increased. Severity sev2. Resolved in 64.8 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-09-03 03:23 UTC: Correctable ECC errors on GPU 3 rose to 220 in 30 minutes; remapped rows increased.
- 03:51 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-09-05 20:10 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degrading HBM stack on GPU 3 (serial 1666868110457).

## Resolution
Reset applied pending row remaps; errors resumed within 2 days; GPU replaced under RMA.

## Lessons
Errors resuming after a reset is the RMA signal; don't keep resetting.
