# inc-2025039: NVLink errors between GPUs 2-3 on h100-node-04

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on h100-node-04 dropped 18%. Severity sev2. Resolved in 57.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-11-29 08:25 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 2; training throughput on h100-node-04 dropped 18%.
- 08:33 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-12-01 18:20 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty NVSwitch port on the baseboard.

## Resolution
Errors returned after reset; baseboard replaced by the vendor.

## Lessons
Attach nvidia-smi nvlink -e counters from both GPUs to the RMA.
