# inc-2025018: NVLink errors between GPUs 4-5 on a100-node-02

## Summary
NVLink CRC errors climbed to 128 per minute between GPUs 4-5; all-reduce time doubled. Severity sev2. Resolved in 26.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-08-11 04:58 UTC: NVLink CRC errors climbed to 128 per minute between GPUs 4-5; all-reduce time doubled.
- 05:32 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-08-12 07:49 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty NVSwitch port on the baseboard.

## Resolution
Errors returned after reset; baseboard replaced by the vendor.

## Lessons
Attach nvidia-smi nvlink -e counters from both GPUs to the RMA.
