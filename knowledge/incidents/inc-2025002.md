# inc-2025002: Power capping on h100-node-01 after PSU loss

## Summary
Jobs on h100-node-01 slowed by 35%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit. Severity sev2. Resolved in 6.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-12 13:43 UTC: Jobs on h100-node-01 slowed by 35%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit.
- 13:52 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-12 19:41 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Power cap left in place after a facility event the week before.

## Resolution
Removed the manual cap with nvidia-smi -pl; no hardware fault.

## Lessons
Record every manual cap with an end condition; forgotten caps look like PSU faults.
