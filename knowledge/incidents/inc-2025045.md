# inc-2025045: GPU 5 fell off the bus on h100-node-01

## Summary
XID 79 on GPU 5; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-01 failed. Severity sev1. Resolved in 32.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-06-02 18:24 UTC: XID 79 on GPU 5; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-01 failed.
- 19:03 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-06-04 03:05 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty PCIe riser for GPU 5.

## Resolution
Node rebooted, riser replaced after a second XID 79 a week later.

## Lessons
PCIe replays had been rising on this GPU for days before the first XID 79.
