# inc-2025007: Thermal throttling on h100-node-04 GPUs 4-7

## Summary
BMC reported FAN4 at 0 RPM; GPUs 4-7 reached 86 C and entered thermal slowdown. Severity sev2. Resolved in 2.3 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-08-13 06:48 UTC: BMC reported FAN4 at 0 RPM; GPUs 4-7 reached 86 C and entered thermal slowdown.
- 07:16 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-08-13 09:04 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN4 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN4, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
