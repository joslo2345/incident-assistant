# inc-2025061: PCIe replays on a100-node-03 GPU 2

## Summary
PCIe replay counter on GPU 2 rising 121 per minute; data loading slowed. Severity sev3. Resolved in 15.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-22 03:02 UTC: PCIe replay counter on GPU 2 rising 121 per minute; data loading slowed.
- 03:33 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-22 18:01 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Riser not fully seated after maintenance.

## Resolution
Reseated the riser; link back at x16 Gen5, counter flat.

## Lessons
Check LnkSta against LnkCap after every service visit.
