# inc-2025037: Power capping on h100-node-01 after PSU loss

## Summary
Jobs on h100-node-01 slowed by 27%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit. Severity sev3. Resolved in 7.1 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-01-21 09:09 UTC: Jobs on h100-node-01 slowed by 27%; every GPU showed sw_power_cap at a reduced limit.
- 09:40 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-01-21 16:17 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Power cap left in place after a facility event the week before.

## Resolution
Removed the manual cap with nvidia-smi -pl; no hardware fault.

## Lessons
Record every manual cap with an end condition; forgotten caps look like PSU faults.
