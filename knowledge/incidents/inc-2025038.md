# inc-2025038: GPU 3 fell off the bus on h100-node-02

## Summary
XID 79 on GPU 3; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-02 failed. Severity sev1. Resolved in 24.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-10-19 16:01 UTC: XID 79 on GPU 3; the GPU stopped reporting and all jobs on h100-node-02 failed.
- 16:37 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-10-20 16:45 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Faulty PCIe riser for GPU 3.

## Resolution
Node rebooted, riser replaced after a second XID 79 a week later.

## Lessons
PCIe replays had been rising on this GPU for days before the first XID 79.
