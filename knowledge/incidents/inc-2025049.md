# inc-2025049: Thermal throttling on h100-node-02 GPUs 0-3

## Summary
Detector flagged GPUs 0-3 running 7 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected. Severity sev2. Resolved in 3.0 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-03-02 10:36 UTC: Detector flagged GPUs 0-3 running 7 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected.
- 11:00 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-03-02 13:39 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN2 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN2, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
