# inc-2025019: PCIe replays on h100-node-01 GPU 6

## Summary
GPU 6 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing. Severity sev3. Resolved in 11.3 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-07-14 01:15 UTC: GPU 6 trained at x8 instead of x16 after a reboot; replays climbing.
- 01:31 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-07-14 12:30 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Degraded PCIe cable to the retimer.

## Resolution
Replaced the cable; replays stopped.

## Lessons
Replays on one GPU only point at its own path, not the PCIe switch.
