# inc-2025004: NVLink errors between GPUs 4-5 on h100-node-01

## Summary
XID 74 on GPU 4; training throughput on h100-node-01 dropped 34%. Severity sev2. Resolved in 38.7 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-02-17 10:57 UTC: XID 74 on GPU 4; training throughput on h100-node-01 dropped 34%.
- 11:07 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-02-19 01:42 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Link training issue cleared by a baseboard reset.

## Resolution
Drained, reset all GPUs on the baseboard; errors did not return.

## Lessons
On NVSwitch systems, reset the whole baseboard, not one GPU.
