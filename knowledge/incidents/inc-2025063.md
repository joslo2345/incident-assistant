# inc-2025063: Thermal throttling on a100-node-01 GPUs 4-7

## Summary
Detector flagged GPUs 4-7 running 9 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected. Severity sev3. Resolved in 2.9 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-12-01 03:38 UTC: Detector flagged GPUs 4-7 running 9 C hotter than their power predicts; no BMC entry was collected.
- 03:58 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-12-01 06:35 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Failed fan module FAN4 (bearing failure).

## Resolution
Drained the node, hot-swapped FAN4, burn-in passed.

## Lessons
The thermal residual alert fired before throttling started; acting on it saves a job restart.
