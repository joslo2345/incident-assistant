# inc-2025062: Suspected overheating on h100-node-03 (no fault)

## Summary
Detector raised a temperature anomaly on GPUs 4-7; utilization went from idle to 99%. Severity sev4. Resolved in 0.6 hours.

## Timeline
- 2025-03-24 13:50 UTC: Detector raised a temperature anomaly on GPUs 4-7; utilization went from idle to 99%.
- 14:02 UTC: on-call acknowledged and started diagnosis.
- 2025-03-24 14:27 UTC: resolved.

## Root cause
Another team's job was sharing the node and saturating the GPUs.

## Resolution
Moved the job to exclusive nodes via the scheduler; no hardware action.

## Lessons
Noisy neighbors are a scheduling problem, not a hardware one.
